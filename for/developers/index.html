What docenta does for developers

Measured against grep.

On 227 judged questions over a real machine, the semantic tool put the truth in the top ten for 114; ripgrep managed 44 and read 2.7 MB per question to get there against 20 KB.

Code-aware retrieval.

A repository map, co-change history and claims per module. The path lane knows a filename-shaped question when it sees one. Sessions and commits are searchable the same way.

Built for the agent, not a search box.

content_search, semantic_search, evidence_pack, entity_card and timelines over MCP. Every page discloses what was searched, what was dropped and how many documents held every term.

Numbers from docs/eval in the docenta repository, reproduced by the head-to-head rig. Nothing here is a benchmark score of a model.
