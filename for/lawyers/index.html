What is in the collection

The United States Code.

All 54 titles, section by section, in the current text published by the House Office of the Law Revision Counsel, with the public laws of recent Congresses from GovInfo beside them.

Two open contract collections.

CUAD, 510 commercial contracts with expert clause annotations from The Atticus Project, and ContractNLI, non-disclosure agreements with their annotated hypotheses. Both CC BY 4.0, both cited by name.

What it is not.

Federal statutes and contracts only. It holds no case law, no regulations (CFR, Federal Register), no state law and no legal advice. Read the official text at uscode.house.gov or govinfo.gov before you rely on a section.

docenta finds and cites; your assistant reads and you decide. When a question falls outside the collection, the answer says so instead of guessing.

Plug it into your assistant

One address, one key, every question cited.

The collection is served as an MCP server, the connector standard Claude, ChatGPT and most coding assistants speak. You add the address once; from then on your assistant asks it the way it asks any other tool.

Ask for a key

Use the form at the bottom of this page and say "lawyers". You get back a key, a long line of letters and numbers. Keep it like a password: it is what lets your assistant in.

Claude Code: one command

Open a terminal and paste this line, with your key in place of the words in angle brackets. Then start a new session; the collection shows up as docenta-legal.

claude mcp add --transport http docenta-legal https://lawyers.docenta.ai/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer <your key>"

Claude.ai or ChatGPT: a custom connector

In the settings, under connectors, add a remote MCP server with the address https://lawyers.docenta.ai/mcp. Where it asks for a header or a token, enter Authorization: Bearer <your key>. Save, then ask your question in a new chat.

Ask, and read the citation

Every answer names the section or the contract it came from. The first question after a quiet spell can take half a minute while the collection wakes up; your assistant is told so and asks again by itself.

What the door does not do: it never sees your files, it holds nothing you send beyond the question you ask, and it cannot be changed by anyone, us included, without a new seal and a new date on this page.

And on your own machine: the matter file

Exhibits, not summaries.

Every answer cites the file, the page and the span. A scanned exhibit is read by the OCR lane and cited the same way. When the collection does not hold the answer, docenta says so instead of improvising.

The whole matter, including mail.

PDF, Word, scans, the 32 GB mailbox export and the live mailbox. Attachments are members of their message, so "the version she sent" is a question, not a hunt.

Privilege stays on the machine.

No upload, no cloud index, no telemetry. The index is stored beside your files, and everything docenta derives is exportable at any time, in every license state.

docenta is retrieval infrastructure beneath your review tools, not a replacement for them. It finds and cites; you read and decide.
