What docenta does for people who live in mail

Fields, not just words.

from:, to:, subject:, dates and a sent/received direction are first-class. Numbers match in every spelling. "The newest message about X" is one query.

Threads and attachments as one.

A hit knows its thread; the thread comes back in order. An attachment carries its message, so the invoice and the promise are found together.

Setup in four plain steps.

docenta opens a page, walks you through making a key at your mail provider, and stores it in the Keychain. From then on new mail arrives on its own, every night.

Gmail, iCloud and Yahoo mail today. Outlook.com is not yet possible: Microsoft allows no password access to personal mail.
