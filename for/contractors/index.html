What is in the collection

The City's rules.

The Palo Alto Municipal Code (Supp. 81, 2026; Titles 8, 12, 16 and 18 among them), the Building Division and Green Building handouts, Ordinance 5412 on accessory dwelling units, the Tree Technical Manual and the trees-and-ADUs guideline.

The utility and the state.

CPAU's electric service requirements, rules 3 and 20, connection fees and the application packet; the California ADU statute (Gov. Code 66310 to 66342) and HCD's ADU handbook of March 2026.

The building code, both editions.

Title 24 as published for 2022 (the edition that governs permits deemed complete before 2026) and 2025, part by part, so the answer names the edition that applies.

docenta finds and cites; your assistant reads and you decide. The collection holds no case law and no permit records, and when a question falls outside it, the answer says so instead of guessing. For anything load-bearing, confirm against the official text the citation points to.

Plug it into your assistant

One address, one key, every question cited.

The collection is served as an MCP server, the connector standard Claude, ChatGPT and most coding assistants speak. You add the address once; from then on your assistant asks it the way it asks any other tool.

Ask for a key

Use the form at the bottom of this page and say "contractors". You get back a key, a long line of letters and numbers. Keep it like a password: it is what lets your assistant in.

Claude Code: one command

Open a terminal and paste this line, with your key in place of the words in angle brackets. Then start a new session; the collection shows up as docenta-palo-alto.

claude mcp add --transport http docenta-palo-alto https://door.docenta.ai/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer <your key>"

Claude.ai or ChatGPT: a custom connector

In the settings, under connectors, add a remote MCP server with the address https://door.docenta.ai/mcp. Where it asks for a header or a token, enter Authorization: Bearer <your key>. Save, then ask your question in a new chat.

Ask, and read the citation

Every answer names the document and the page it came from. The first question after a quiet spell can take half a minute while the collection wakes up; your assistant is told so and asks again by itself.

What the door does not do: it never sees your files, it holds nothing you send beyond the question you ask, and it cannot be changed by anyone, us included, without a new seal and a new date on this page.
