What docenta does for researchers

Public benchmarks, published.

BEIR SciFact nDCG@10 of 0.72 with the dense channel and 0.66 lexical, within a point of the published baselines; LongMemEval evidence recall@5 of 0.97. The rigs are in the repository.

Conflicts shown, gaps disclosed.

The evidence pack carries the route it took, the cited spans, the conflicts between sources and the questions the collection cannot answer. A PDF whose text reads out of order is flagged, never quoted blind.

Across languages, across years.

Dense retrieval finds the German paper from an English question. Entity timelines put a concept's history in order across papers, notes and mail.

docenta is retrieval, not a model. Pair it with the model you already use; it hands over the evidence and the receipts.
